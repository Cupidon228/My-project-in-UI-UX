## 🇬🇧 English Version

# DUITZ Military Banner 2026

## About The Project

This banner was created for the DUITZ 2026 admission campaign focused on military personnel and veterans.

The main goal is to attract attention to modern IT education, cybersecurity, and digital technologies.

---

## Banner Goals

- Promoting the 2026 admission campaign
- Supporting IT education awareness
- Presenting DUITZ visually
- Highlighting cybersecurity and digital technologies
- Creating a modern military-tech identity

---

## Main Advantages

- Admission without NMT
- State-funded education opportunities
- Officer career pathway
- Flexible learning format
- Modern laboratories
- Cyber ranges
- Practical education

---

## Design Concept

- Dark military-tech style
- Cybersecurity atmosphere
- Blue neon accents
- Modern typography
- Minimalistic UI

---

- Canva
